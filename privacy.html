Privacy Policy

Privacy, kept simple.

Not There Yet is designed for private, shared-device family play. Gameplay data stays on your device, and the app does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Privacy by design

Not There Yet does not collect through the app:

  • User accounts
  • Email addresses
  • Personal profiles
  • Location data
  • Analytics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage tracking
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • Cloud gameplay data

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Adventure history
  • Road Crew information
  • Player statistics
  • Game progress
  • App preferences

Not There Yet does not upload this information or share it with third parties.

Apple device and iCloud backups

Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, locally stored app data may be included in an Apple-managed device backup or iCloud Backup. Not There Yet does not upload, access, control, or manage those backups.

Apple backups are separate from app-provided cloud synchronization. Not There Yet does not run a cloud gameplay service or sync gameplay between devices.

Removing your data

Deleting Not There Yet removes its locally stored data from that device. Restoring a previous Apple device or iCloud backup may also restore app data that was included in that backup.

Device permissions

Not There Yet does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Apple App Store

When you download, install, purchase, or review Not There Yet through the Apple App Store, Apple may process information under its own privacy policies. Apple collects and manages that information; Not There Yet does not receive or control it.

Support email

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and only the information you include. We use it to answer your question, troubleshoot an issue, or review feedback.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The website itself does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. GitHub may process limited technical request information, such as your IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, and access times, to deliver and protect the site. GitHub manages that information under its own privacy statement.

Children’s privacy

Not There Yet is designed for families to play together. Because the app does not create accounts or collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Privacy contact

If you have questions about privacy, please contact questplatformapps@gmail.com.

Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Not There Yet evolves. This page will always show the latest version.
