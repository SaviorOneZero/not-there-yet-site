Privacy Policy

Privacy, kept simple.

Not There Yet keeps its data on your device. This page explains what stays there and what happens if you choose to email support.

What the app does not collect

  • No analytics
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No accounts
  • No location collection
  • No remote configuration

What stays on your device

Road Crew names, Adventure progress, player stats, and Adventure Log details stay on your device. Nothing is sent elsewhere.

Support email

If you email support, we receive only what you choose to send. We use it to answer your question, troubleshoot an issue, or review feedback.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, please contact questplatformapps@gmail.com.
