Age Suitability

Made for families and mixed ages.

Not There Yet is designed for families and mixed-age groups to play together on one shared device.

Content and reading

Not There Yet contains no in-app purchases or unrestricted web access. Some games include written prompts, so younger players may need an adult or older player to read them aloud.

Shared family play

The app is designed for adults and children to play together on the same device. There is no online chat, social networking, user-generated content, or in-app advertising. Gameplay is local to the shared iPhone or iPad.

A simple privacy reassurance

Not There Yet does not require accounts and does not collect personal information from children. It also uses no ads, analytics, or tracking. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

For parents and guardians

Parents and guardians should decide what is appropriate for their family and supervise device use while traveling.
