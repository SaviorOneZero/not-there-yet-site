About Not There Yet

Made for the miles between leaving and finally getting there.

Not There Yet is built around a simple idea: road trips are better when everyone has a reason to look up. Six included games turn every drive into an adventure built around spotting things, calling them out, and noticing the world outside.

How families play

Set up your Road Crew once, then play together on one shared device. When you come back, Home makes it easy to resume an active Adventure or start another with the people who played last time. Confirm who is along, replay a favorite, get a surprise pick, or choose from all six games.

Everyone gets a colorful game piece, and each new Adventure receives a friendly name that you can change from its dashboard. When the ride ends, Adventure Complete shows the Road Crew and games played before the result is saved to the Adventure Log. Everything works offline on iPhone and iPad.

What we care about

  • Easy enough to start while the car is already moving
  • Good for mixed ages, short attention spans, and real family trips
  • Shared play that keeps the focus on the people in the car and the road outside

Road trips that fit the region

Not There Yet includes road-trip content for the United States and Great Britain. Choose your Road Trip Region in Settings, and new Adventures use familiar plates, vehicles, spelling, terminology, and prompts. In Great Britain, License Plate Challenge becomes Number Plate Challenge.

Region selection does not use your location. Like the rest of the app, it works offline and stays on the shared iPhone or iPad.
