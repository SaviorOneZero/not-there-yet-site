FAQ

Quick answers before the next drive.

Not There Yet keeps family play simple: six games, one shared device, no account required, and offline play wherever the road takes you.

Frequently asked questions

What is Not There Yet?

Not There Yet is a collection of six family road-trip games with quick things to spot, call out, and play along the way.

Which games are included?

License Plate Challenge, Alphabet Hunt, Vehicle Bingo, Roadside Oddities, Road Chain, and Roadside Challenge are all included. With Great Britain selected, License Plate Challenge is presented as Number Plate Challenge.

Which road-trip regions are supported?

Not There Yet includes content for the United States and Great Britain. Choose the Road Trip Region in Settings, and new Adventures use matching plates, vehicles, terminology, and prompts. An active Adventure keeps the region it started with.

Is Not There Yet available in the UK?

Yes. Not There Yet is available from the UK App Store, with a Great Britain road-trip region designed around familiar number plates, vehicles, wording, and roadside prompts.

How do I resume an Adventure?

If an Adventure is in progress, open Not There Yet and choose Resume Adventure on the Home screen. Your Road Crew, completed games, and any supported saved game progress will be waiting.

Can I reuse the same Road Crew?

Yes. Not There Yet remembers the people who played last time. Choose Start with Your Road Crew, confirm who is along for this Adventure, and then replay a favorite, get a surprise pick, or choose a game.

Can I name an Adventure?

Every new Adventure receives a friendly name automatically so you can start quickly. You can rename the active Adventure from its dashboard.

Which devices are supported?

Not There Yet is designed for the whole family to play together on one shared device—an iPhone or iPad. It requires iOS 16 or later on iPhone and iPadOS 16 or later on iPad.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Every game works without an internet connection. Your Road Crew, Adventure progress, and scores stay on your device. Choosing a road-trip region does not require location access or an internet connection.

Does the app collect personal data?

No. Not There Yet does not use analytics, ads, tracking, accounts, location collection, cloud synchronization, or external gameplay services.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

No. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

Are Road Crew names or Adventure Log details sent anywhere?

No. Road Crew names, Adventure progress, player stats, and Adventure Log details stay on the device.

Does everyone need their own device?

No. Everyone riding along can play together on one shared device.

Is it for kids?

It is made for family road trips and mixed ages. A parent or adult should still decide what fits their family, especially while traveling.

Can I send feedback?

Yes. Email questplatformapps@gmail.com. Nothing is sent automatically. We only receive feedback when you choose to email us.

Is Not There Yet affiliated with brands mentioned in gameplay prompts?

Not There Yet may mention common real-world vehicles, roadside objects, or delivery trucks in spotting prompts. These references are for gameplay only. Not There Yet is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any third-party brand mentioned in the app.
